Game Overview
Working Title: Life of P 
Based on: The Island of Four Ps
Genre: Open-zone Action RPG
Perspective: Third-person
Platform: PC (controller supported)
Target Audience: ages 16 - 25 
Core Fantasy:
The player explores a world where ideas are magic; debating, studying, and reshaping them to discover what they truly believe in and want to become.

High Concept
In a world where ideas take magical form, the player journeys through the Island of the Four Ps, learning and making use of their magical spells. By engaging with passions, principles, partners, plans and their darker counterparts, the player learns how ideas evolve and how unchecked ideas can become dangerous. Progression is driven not just by combat, but by understanding. 
Features: 
Adventure through a village open zone, experience the story of the island of four ps with dialogue and actions from the characters, engage in side stories throughout the village, and enjoy magical engaging combat against other adventurers. 
Art Style: 
Low poly artstyle like final fantasy 7 with high quality textures. Villages and magical spells with designs symbolizing their concepts.
Music/Sound: 
Dynamic music that shifts based on the idea density in the neighborhood, whether it's good or bad.

Story and Narrative
World Premise
In this world, ideas do not simply exist in the mind. They manifest into spells of perfect ideas from another realm. Every belief, desire, or habit has the potential to materialize as a magical spell. Find the magical spell set that works for you and prove yourself against the eidos of P.
The Island of the Four Ps is divided into major regions, each representing a fundamental category of ideas. These regions are not just locations, but philosophies made physical.
Narrative Structure
Semi-linear progression through major regions
Open exploration within villages (zones)
Story advances after completing each major “P” area
Dialogue, environmental storytelling, and battles communicate theme
Characters
Guide Character
Archimedes
Archimedes serves as the player’s guide and mentor. He introduces the rules of the world, explains the nature of magic, and functions as the tutorial anchor for each new system.
Play Character
Base character is androgynous 
Begins with a base spell
Neutral, adaptable idea type
Evolves based on the realm types the player prioritizes.
Represents curiosity, growth, and self-discovery
Spells (Idea Manifest)
Physical manifestations of ideas
Each spell represents a specific concept (e.g., Passion, Discipline, Addiction)
Can morph or upgrade into new forms
Example: One passion can evolve into another passion
NPCs
Also practice magic, engage in their passions.
Can provide side quests for each realm.
Could battle you with magic.
Area Bosses
Each major “P” region culminates in a concept boss
Example:
Village of Passions → Boss: Passion itself
Boss battles require the player to sufficiently understand the ideas in that region
GamePlay
Core Gameplay Loop
Explore an open village zone
Experience main quest with Archimedes
Battle or debate ideas in real time
Upgrade realm stats by participating in activities throughout the day.
Manifest and upgrade spells.
Progress through the days doing steps 2-5.
Confront the region’s core concept boss on the final day.
Advance the story

Combat & Debate System
Real-time battles mixed with turn-based abilities 
Combat is framed as debate rather than pure violence
The more times a spell is encountered:
The higher the chance to study it
Studying unlocks deeper understanding and manifest ability
Studying 
To learn a spell:
The player must understand it
The player must possess the correct idea type needed to contain it 
Encourages repeated engagement, not brute force

Tessamark System
The Tessamark is a core progression mechanic
Allows the player to:
Use magic.
Upgrade magic.
Required to unlock boss encounters
Tessamark must be filled to a certain threshold through debates 




Levels & Environments
Villages (Open Zones)
Each village represents a category of ideas 
Open exploration encouraged
Neighborhoods vary in tone:
Safe, curious, lively areas

Village of Passions

Progression Flow
Village of Passions

Journey to Village of Principles 
Additional Regions follow the structure of the Four Ps



Art Direction
Eidos
Semi-abstract, symbolic character designs
Eidos visually communicate their idea
Hostile Eidos appear warped or unstable
Villages feel alive, crowded with concepts
Dark neighborhoods feel tense

Audio
Music shifts based on idea density
Calm, curious tones in open villages
Distorted, uneasy soundscapes in hostile neighborhoods
Boss encounters emphasize conceptual weight, not aggression

Assets
Low-poly assets with high-poly baked on
High resolution textures
Base character model to be easily reused
